About Nilambur

Nilambur is a picturesque town situated on the banks of the Chaliyar River in the Malappuram district of Kerala, India. Known worldwide for its extensive forests teeming with bamboos, teak, rosewood, and mahogany, Nilambur is a treasure trove of biodiversity and a haven for eco-tourism lovers. From the world's oldest teak plantation to enchanting waterfalls, Nilambur offers a ravishing natural beauty.

Top Attractions

Nilambur Teak Museum

Famous for being the world's first teak museum, showcasing the rich heritage, scientific aspects, and aesthetic detail of teak wood. The museum features the root system of a mature teak tree as a welcoming exhibit.

Conolly's Plot

The oldest teak plantation in the world, created between 1842 and 1844 by H.V. Conolly. It is home to Kannimara, the oldest living teak tree, and features a beautiful hanging bridge across the Chaliyar River.

Adyanpara Waterfalls

Located 10 km from Nilambur in the Kumbalangod Village, this cascading waterfall is flanked by a thick cover of lush green forest. The water here is naturally purified and believed to hold mystic medicinal properties.

Nedumkayam Rainforest

Situated 18 km from the town, this sprawling rainforest is an eco-tourism hotspot. It features British-era wooden rest houses, rich biodiversity, and an elephant taming center.

Valamthode Waterfalls

A hilltop destination perfect for trekking, located about 27 km from Nilambur. It is also home to one of the most primal tribes of Kerala, the Chola Naikas.

Central Forest Nursery

A haven for plant lovers, this central forest nursery produces high-quality planting stock of important plantation species like teak, eucalyptus, and acacias.
